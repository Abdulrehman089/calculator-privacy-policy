Privacy Policy

When you use Calculator, you can trust that your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Your privacy matters

Calculator is designed with privacy in mind. The application does not require an account and its core calculation features do not require you to provide personal information.

Information we collect

Calculator does not intentionally collect personal information from users. You can use the core features of the application without creating an account or providing personal information.

No account required

Calculator does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

✓

No personal information

We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or similar personal information.

Information stored on your device

Calculator may store certain information locally on your device to provide application features and remember your preferences.

Calculation history

Your calculation history may be stored locally on your device so that you can review previous calculations and reuse them when needed.

Your calculations stay on your device

Calculation history is not intentionally transmitted to our servers or stored in a remote database.

Application preferences

Calculator may remember settings such as your selected appearance mode and sound-effect preference. These settings are stored locally and are used only to provide the application's functionality.

How information is used

Any information stored locally by Calculator is used to provide the features of the application.

  • Perform mathematical calculations
  • Display calculation history
  • Remember light or dark appearance preferences
  • Remember sound-effect preferences
  • Provide a consistent application experience

Internet and third-party services

The core functionality of Calculator is designed to work without requiring an internet connection.

Calculator does not currently require users to connect an account or submit personal information to use the calculator, history, appearance, or sound-effect features.

If third-party services are added to the application in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain the information those services process.

Advertising

Calculator does not currently display advertisements and does not intentionally use advertising services to collect information about users.

Children's privacy

Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The application does not require users to provide personal information to use its core features.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the application.

Because Calculator does not maintain a remote database of users' calculation history or personal information, the majority of application data remains under the control of the user's device.

Deleting your information

You can delete locally stored calculation history using the available controls within the application.

You can also remove application data through your Android device settings or by uninstalling Calculator.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Calculator, its features, or applicable privacy requirements.

When changes are made, the effective date displayed at the beginning of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

Contact us

Questions about privacy?

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the Calculator application, you can contact the developer through the contact information available on the application's Google Play Store listing.

About Calculator

Calculator is a simple and user-friendly Android application designed to make everyday calculations quick and convenient.

The application includes basic calculations, calculation history, light and dark appearance modes, and optional sound effects.
