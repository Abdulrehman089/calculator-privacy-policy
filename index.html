Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 August 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Calculator handles information when you use our application. We designed Calculator to provide a simple and private calculation experience.

Information We Collect

Calculator does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the application.

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photographs, or other identifying information.

Calculation History

Calculator provides a calculation history feature. Your calculation history is stored locally on your device so that you can view and reuse previous calculations.

Your calculation history stays on your device.

We do not receive or store your calculation history on our servers.

App Preferences

The application may save certain preferences locally on your device, such as your selected appearance mode and sound-effect preference. These settings are used only to provide the application's features.

Device Information

Calculator does not intentionally collect personal device information or use your device information to identify you.

Internet Connection

Calculator's core calculation features are designed to work without requiring an internet connection. We do not transmit your calculations or personal information to a remote server.

Third-Party Services

Calculator does not currently use third-party services that collect personal information from users.

Advertising

Calculator does not currently display advertisements and does not use advertising services to collect information about users.

Children's Privacy

Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Data Security

Since Calculator does not collect or transmit personal information, we do not maintain a server containing your personal information. Information stored locally by the application remains on your device and is subject to the security of your device.

Data Deletion

Because calculation history and preferences are stored locally, you can remove this locally stored information by using the application's available controls or by uninstalling the application from your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements. When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Calculator, you can contact us through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.

About Calculator

Calculator is designed to provide basic calculations, calculation history, light and dark appearance modes, and optional sound effects through a simple and user-friendly interface.
