Privacy Policy

Effective 20 August 2026

When you use Calculator, you can trust that your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Calculator application handles, why it is used, and how you can manage your information.

Information that Calculator collects

Calculator is designed to provide calculation features without requiring users to create an account or submit personal information.

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photographs, or other information that directly identifies you.

Information stored on your device

Some Calculator features require information to be stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the application's features.

Calculation history

Your calculation history is stored locally on your device. It allows you to view previous calculations and reuse them when needed.

Your calculation history is not sent to our servers.

We do not receive, access, or store your calculation history remotely.

Application preferences

Calculator may store preferences such as your selected light or dark appearance mode and sound-effect setting. These preferences remain on your device and are used to provide the application's functionality.

Your apps, browser and device

Calculator does not intentionally collect device information for the purpose of identifying you.

The application does not require access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, or personal files for its core calculation features.

How Calculator uses information

Information stored locally by Calculator is used only to provide the features you choose to use, including:

  • Performing calculations
  • Displaying calculation history
  • Remembering appearance preferences
  • Remembering sound-effect preferences

Internet and online services

The core features of Calculator are designed to work without requiring an internet connection. Your calculations and locally stored preferences are not transmitted to our servers.

Third-party services

Calculator does not currently use third-party services that collect personal information from users.

Advertising

Calculator does not currently display advertisements and does not use advertising services to collect information about users.

Children's privacy

Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The application does not require users to provide personal information to use its core features.

Security

Because Calculator does not collect or transmit personal information to our servers, we do not maintain a database of your personal information. Information stored locally by the application is protected by the security features of your device.

Deleting information

You can remove locally stored calculation history using the application's available controls. You can also remove locally stored application data through your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its features, or applicable requirements.

When we make changes, the updated effective date will be displayed at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

Contact us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Calculator, please contact us through the contact information provided on the Calculator application’s Google Play Store listing.

About Calculator

Calculator is a simple and user-friendly calculator application that provides basic calculations, calculation history, light and dark modes, and optional sound effects.
