Privacy Policy for Calculator

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Calculator ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

Calculator does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its features.

Calculation history is stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to us or shared with third parties.

App preferences, such as sound effects and appearance settings, are used to provide the requested functionality. These settings remain on your device.

Third-Party Services

The App does not currently use third-party services that collect personal information from users.

Children's Privacy

Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information, no personal information is stored on our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us
